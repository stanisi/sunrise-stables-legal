Privacy policy

Sunrise Stables (com.stanisi.sunrisestables)
Effective 20 September 2026. Last updated 20 September 2026.

Who we are

This policy is for the mobile game Sunrise Stables, published by Stanisi Games (“we”, “us”). It describes how the app handles information when you play on Android / Google Play.

Contact: stanisigames@gmail.com

What the game stores on your device

Progress, lives, coins, boosters, horses, settings (including music and effects volume), and similar gameplay state are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this local copy.

Analytics (GameAnalytics)

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played (for example level starts, wins, fails, and in-game currency earned or spent). The SDK creates a random User ID. You can view and copy it in the app under Settings.

GameAnalytics may process device and usage information (such as device type, OS version, country/region, session length, and the events above). We do not use it to ask for your name, and we do not sell player lists.

Their notices: GameAnalytics privacy.

Google Play Games and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, we may store a cloud snapshot of your progress (saved games named for Sunrise Stables) so you can continue on another device. Sign-in is optional; the game still works with a local save if you skip it.

Google processes your Google account, Play Games profile, and related data under Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional in-app items (coins and packs) are sold through Google Play Billing when that is enabled. Google handles payment details. We receive that a product was purchased so we can grant the items in the game. We do not see your full card number.

What we do not do (today)

  • We do not show third-party ads in the current version.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not require an email or password of our own to play.

Children

Sunrise Stables is a casual horse match-3. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided data, email us and we will delete what we control.

This paragraph must match what you declare in Play Console under Target audience. If you later mark the app as for children, you must tighten analytics, purchases, and this policy.

Why we process data

  • Run the game and remember progress (local save and optional cloud save).
  • Fix bugs and balance levels (analytics).
  • Deliver optional shop items (Play Billing).

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, these purposes are typically our legitimate interest in operating the game, and/or performance of a purchase. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object, depending on your region.

How long we keep it

Local data stays until you uninstall or clear app storage. Cloud save stays until you overwrite it, delete it via Google, or we delete it after a valid request. Analytics are kept by GameAnalytics according to their retention practices until we request erasure for your User ID.

Your choices

  • Play without Play Games sign-in (no cloud snapshot from us).
  • Turn down or mute music and effects in Settings (this does not stop analytics).
  • Uninstall to remove the on-device save.
  • Request deletion of analytics / cloud save we control: Data deletion request.

Changes

If we add ads, new login, or new data uses, we will update this page and the “last updated” date. Continued use after a material change means the new policy applies from that date.
